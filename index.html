The studio

Larp Studio is a small web development team working out of Algeria. We keep projects lean by design — fewer handoffs, faster builds, and one point of contact from first message to launch.

  • Design and build, togetherLayout, content, and functionality are handled as one process, not passed between separate people.
  • Direct communicationYou talk to the person actually building your site — no account managers relaying messages.
  • Small by choiceWe take on a limited number of projects at a time so each one gets real attention.

How a project runs

4 steps

A short, predictable flow from the first message to a site you fully own and can walk away with.

  • 01
    Scope, in writingPages, features, and price agreed upfront before anything gets built — no surprise additions later.
  • 02
